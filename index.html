SafeSpend: Between Paydays

SafeSpend is a private budgeting app for iPhone that tells you what's safe to spend today, keeps your bills and savings set aside first and never sends your data anywhere.

Frequently asked questions

Does SafeSpend connect to my bank?
No. You enter income and expenses yourself, or let a Shortcuts automation add each Apple Pay payment for you. Nothing is ever downloaded from a bank, and there is no account to create.
Where is my data stored?
Only on your iPhone. SafeSpend has no servers, no analytics and no cloud sync. Its data is part of your normal iPhone backup, so restoring a backup on a new iPhone brings it back.
When does my financial month start?
When your money arrives, not necessarily on the 1st. You pick the start day during onboarding and can change it for the next month in More. A month can start from expected income, your current balance, or both.
What is “Safe to spend today”?
Your remaining budget for this financial month – after planned payments and your savings target are set aside – spread over the days left until payday. Spend more than today's amount and tomorrow's amount adjusts so you still make it to payday.
How do I export my transactions?
More → Export data → Export CSV. The file is created on your iPhone and goes only where you share or save it. Cards are never exported.
I forgot my passcode.
The passcode is kept only in your iPhone's Keychain and cannot be recovered or reset remotely. The only way back in is to erase SafeSpend's data from the lock screen and start again.
How do I delete everything?
More → Reset removes all of SafeSpend's local data. Deleting the app does the same.
