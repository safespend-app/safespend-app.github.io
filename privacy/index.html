Privacy Policy

SafeSpend: Between Paydays · Last updated 21 October 2026

The short version

SafeSpend collects no data. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone, and it leaves only when you choose to export or share it.

1. Scope

This policy explains what the SafeSpend app does with your information. It applies to the SafeSpend app for iPhone, including its widgets, Siri and Shortcuts actions.

2. What we collect

Nothing. SafeSpend does not collect, transmit or store any personal data on our side. We do not receive your name, email address, device identifiers, location, usage statistics or anything you type into the app. The app makes no network requests of its own.

3. What stays on your iPhone

To do its job, SafeSpend keeps the following in its own storage on your device:

  • Your financial months: income, starting balance, savings target, planned payments and their status.
  • Your transactions: amounts, categories, dates and optional notes.
  • Your cards: name, barcode or QR code, and a photo, colour or gradient you chose for them.
  • Your settings: currency, start day, reminders, haptics and the passcode lock.

This data is stored only on your iPhone. Like other app data, it is included in your iPhone backups (iCloud Backup or a backup to your computer), which are governed by Apple's privacy policy. We have no way to access it.

4. Permissions and why we ask for them

Camera
Used only when you open the scanner, to read a card's barcode or QR code and to photograph a card. Images are stored on your iPhone and never uploaded.
Face ID and Touch ID
Used to unlock the app when you turn the passcode lock on. The check is done by iOS; SafeSpend learns only whether it was you. Your passcode is kept in a protected form in your iPhone's Keychain and never moves to another device.
Photo library (adding only)
Used only to save an image you chose to share, such as a month recap. SafeSpend never reads your photos.
Notifications
Reminders are optional and scheduled on your iPhone by iOS; there is no notification server. They never show amounts, and with the passcode lock on they do not name payments either.
Siri and Shortcuts
The Add Expense action saves the amount and merchant that Siri, Shortcuts or a Wallet automation gives it, only in SafeSpend on your iPhone. With the passcode lock on, Siri confirms only the amount and the category.

5. Widgets

The Safe to Spend widget shows your amounts and your next planned payment on the Home Screen and Lock Screen. That information is saved only on your iPhone for the widget. A locked iPhone hides it, and with the passcode lock on the widget shows no amounts at all.

6. Exports and sharing

Sharing is always your choice. A CSV export of your transactions and a month recap image are created on your iPhone only when you ask for them, and go only to the app or place you send them. The month recap hides amounts unless you turn them on. Cards are never exported.

7. Third parties, tracking and advertising

SafeSpend contains no third-party SDKs, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind. We do not sell or share data, because we do not have any.

8. Children

SafeSpend is a personal finance tool and is not directed at children. Because the app collects no data, it does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

9. Deleting your data

More → Reset removes all of SafeSpend's data from your iPhone. Deleting the app removes it as well. Because nothing is stored elsewhere, there is nothing for us to delete.

10. Changes to this policy

If SafeSpend ever starts storing or sending data differently, we will update this page and the Privacy Policy screen inside the app (More → Privacy Policy), and say so in the app's release notes.
